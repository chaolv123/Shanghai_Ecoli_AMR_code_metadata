Suggested Results text:
To assess whether the post-2017 temporal patterns were robust to farm-level repeated sampling and geographic structure, we fitted mixed-effects models adjusted for sampling year and district, with farm identity included as a random intercept. In host-stratified models using all farms, significant adjusted post-2017 declines were retained for 18 host-antimicrobial combinations, whereas significant adjusted increases were observed for 2 combinations (Supplementary Table 7).
For MDR, the adjusted post-2017 odds ratios were: Poultry MDR_binary (adjusted OR=0.37, 95% CI 0.184-0.746, P=0.00546); Swine MDR_binary (adjusted OR=0.629, 95% CI 0.22-1.793, P=0.385). For antibiogram length, the adjusted post-2017 rate ratios were: Poultry antibiogram_length (adjusted RR=0.811, 95% CI 0.751-0.875, P=8.37e-08); Swine antibiogram_length (adjusted RR=0.798, 95% CI 0.743-0.856, P=4.29e-10).
A restricted analysis of farms sampled in more than five years included 8 farms and provided a repeated-sampling sensitivity check with the same model structure (Supplementary Table 7).

Suggested Methods text:
As a sensitivity analysis, we fitted generalized mixed-effects models to account for repeated farm-level sampling and geographic structure. Binary resistance outcomes and MDR status were analysed using logistic mixed-effects models, and antibiogram length was analysed using a count mixed-effects model. Models included period, sampling year and district as fixed effects and farm identity as a random intercept; all-host models also included host and a period-by-host interaction. Results are reported as adjusted odds ratios or rate ratios with Wald 95% confidence intervals. We repeated the analysis in the subset of farms sampled in more than five years.

Suggested Response-to-reviewer text:
To strengthen the temporal inference while acknowledging the observational design, we added farm- and district-adjusted sensitivity analyses. Specifically, we fitted generalized mixed-effects models for antimicrobial-specific phenotypic resistance, MDR status and antibiogram length, including sampling year and district as fixed effects and farm identity as a random intercept. The results are summarized in Supplementary Table 7 and show that the main post-2017 declines were broadly retained after accounting for farm-level repeated sampling and geographic structure. We also repeated the analysis among farms sampled in more than five years. We have revised the manuscript to describe these as adjusted temporal associations during the antimicrobial-transition period rather than causal effects of the National Action Plan.

Important limitation:
The supplied metadata.xlsx does not contain WGS isolate indicators or ARG_count; therefore Supplementary Table 7 generated here covers phenotypic resistance, MDR status and antibiogram length, but not ARG burden.
